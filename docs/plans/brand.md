## Positioning and voice

- **Quiet luxury, said out loud.** «А що, якщо купувати менше — це вже новий luxury? … Для нас
  luxury — це не мати більше. Це вибирати краще.» Hashtag `#quietluxury`. The pitch is fewer,
  better things that stay with you — never trend, never discount language (one launch promo:
  «на перші 3 сумки 4800 замість 6800»).
- **Calm, short, second person, no exclamation marks.** Captions are 3–6 short lines: «Без
  зайвого. Без компромісів. Просто шкіра, форма і ваш стиль.» / «Усе необхідне. Нічого
  зайвого.» / «Менше хаосу, більше порядку.» Product first, then one idea about daily life,
  then 📐 size and 💳 price. Mostly «ви», occasionally «ти». The site's returns page («ти»)
  is the odd one out against the rest of the site («ви») — Instagram does both, so it is not
  wrong, just unaligned.
- **Named models, Latin caps.** AVELINE, FORMA, DUAL, Hidden, BASIC, Slimbag, Lunet, Baguette —
  every bag has a one-word name and is referred to by it. The site's catalog uses descriptive
  Ukrainian titles («Сумка на плече») because the photo folder came without names. **Names
  should come back**: `Lunet`, `AVELINE`, `FORMA`, `DUAL`, `Hidden`, `BASIC`, `Baguette` are
  brand assets customers search for.
- **Craft is the proof.** «Майстерність — це те, що не видно, але саме вона створює річ, яку
  видно одразу.» Workshop, sewing machine, embossing stamps, «виробництво 🪡» highlight. The
  about page's founder-in-the-workshop photo is the right hero.
- **Palette as a feature.** «Обирайте свій колір — у нашій палітрі їх значно більше, ніж
  один.» / «робимо в різних кольорах з нашої палітри». Colour variants on the site are
  right; a «палітра» note on product pages («інші кольори — під замовлення») is missing.
- **Contact channel:** Direct and the phone `098 602 66 26` («у будь-якому месенджері») —
  matches `/contacts`.

## Visual identity

- **Logo:** «BÁLIER» in a Garamond-cut serif, wide tracking, over a pebbled-leather circle;
  «bags and accessories» underneath in a light sans. The acute on the Á is part of the mark —
  the site's header wordmark now carries it.
- **Post graphics:** headlines in a high-contrast serif with italics («ТВІЙ колір / ТВІЙ
  стиль», «ЯКУ СУМКУ ОБРАТИ під ВАШ РИТМ ЖИТТЯ?»), captions and CTAs in a plain sans, spaced
  small caps for «BÁLIER × …». Two type roles, serif for voice, sans for information — the
  site now mirrors that: **Cormorant Garamond** (display, wordmark) + **Source Sans 3** (copy).
- **Photography:** two registers. Studio-white product shots with a small «BÁLIER» watermark
  in the corner, and street/lifestyle shots in Dnipro (brick, stone steps, glass façades) with
  the bag worn. Neutral wardrobe: black, cream, camel, denim. The shadcn-neutral site palette
  sits well under this; the old cream-and-rust theme fought the photos.
- **Colours of the leather:** black, dark brown, chocolate, cocoa suede, milky/cream, graphite
  python emboss, plus a lilac lining on the wallets. The catalog's swatches match.

## What to change on the site (proposed, not done)

1. **Prices → the captions' numbers** where a match is certain (Lunet ✓, wallet ✓, AVELINE
   5 500, Baguette 4 200, travel 15 500, backpack 7 500 M / 8 500 L).
2. **Names → the brand's** (`AVELINE`, `Lunet`, `FORMA`, `DUAL`, `Hidden`, `Baguette`, …) with
   the descriptive title kept as the second line / description opener, so search finds both.
3. **Backpack: one product, sizes M and L** — the panel has colour variants only. Cheapest
   honest answer: two variants per colour named «Чорний · M», «Чорний · L» with
   `price_override` on L. No schema change.
4. **Missing lines**: косметичка (2 800), BASIC (2 800), шопер-мішок (4 800), Slimbag (1 700),
   чохол для окулярів (850), ремінь, капці, корсети — the owner decides which are launch
   products; photos exist on Instagram for all of them.
5. **Palette note** on every product page: «Інші кольори з палітри BÁLIER — під замовлення,
   напишіть нам» with the Telegram/phone link. One sentence in the product template.
6. **Personalisation** («Тиснення / Гравіювання») — a line on the product page and a mention
   on `/about`; the checkout option stays Phase 6.
7. **Warranty 3 years** — already on `/returns`; worth one line on `/about` too, next to the
   numbers, since Instagram leads with it.
8. **Copy tone** — the product descriptions in the catalog are already in this register
   (short, second person, no exclamation marks); the returns page's «ти» + exclamation marks
   are the only off-note.
